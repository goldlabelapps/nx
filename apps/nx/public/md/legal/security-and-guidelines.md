# Security & Guidelines

**Effective Date:** September 7, 2026

At Goldlabel Apps Ltd, security, integrity, and trust are fundamental to our engineering process and platform operations.

## 1. Security Infrastructure

We maintain modern security standards across our applications and open-source packages:

- **Encrypted Communications:** All HTTP traffic is strictly enforced over TLS/HTTPS.
- **Dependency Auditing:** Automated scanning for known vulnerabilities across npm dependencies.
- **Access Control:** Principle of least privilege enforced for authentication, database access, and infrastructure management.

## 2. Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure

If you discover a potential security vulnerability within any of our services or open-source software, we encourage you to report it to us responsibly:

- Email details to **goldlabel.apps@gmail.com** with steps to reproduce.
- Allow reasonable time for investigation and remediation before public disclosure.
- Do not exploit the vulnerability to access unauthorized data or degrade system performance.

## 3. Community & Development Guidelines

When contributing to or interacting with Goldlabel Apps Ltd projects:

- Respect privacy and maintain confidentiality.
- Adhere to safe coding practices, avoiding hardcoded secrets or unvalidated inputs.
- Engage professionally and constructively across repository discussions and issues.

## 4. Security Updates & Inquiries

For security inquiries or to report an issue, please contact our team directly at **goldlabel.apps@gmail.com**.
