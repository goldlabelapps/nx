# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 7, 2026

Goldlabel Apps Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclosure, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and applications.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services, when you participate in activities on our site, or when you contact us directly.

- **Personal Data:** Name, email address, contact preferences, and phone number when voluntarily submitted via contact forms or communication channels.
- **Technical Data:** IP address, browser type, device details, operating system, and usage telemetry collected automatically for diagnostic and security purposes.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our website for business purposes described below:

- To provide, operate, and maintain our services.
- To improve, personalize, and expand our platform.
- To communicate with you regarding updates, inquiries, or support requests.
- To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or security vulnerabilities.

## 3. Data Protection & Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. However, please also remember that no system or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers solely to assist in operating our site and delivering services (e.g. hosting, analytics, notification services).
