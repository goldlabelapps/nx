## Why This Document Exists

This overview explains why the Leida repository represents more than a concept. It shows the breadth of execution already completed and why the current MVP phase is building on a credible software foundation.

## Investment-Level Summary

Leida is already supported by:

- a dual-application architecture
- reusable platform infrastructure
- operational admin workflows
- product ingestion and curation capability
- centralized documentation and delivery discipline

In plain terms, the team has already done a meaningful amount of the hidden work that turns an idea into a viable product platform.

## The 5 Ws for an Investor

### What has been built?

A working software foundation with:

- a public-facing product app
- an internal founder dashboard
- shared design and runtime infrastructure
- documented operational workflows

### Why does that matter?

Because early-stage products often appear more mature than they are. In this case, the repository shows underlying depth:

- the system has separated public and internal concerns
- the codebase has shared assets rather than isolated prototypes
- the product has operational logic, not just presentation

### Who does this serve?

- end users
- internal operators
- future technical hires
- external stakeholders evaluating growth readiness

### Where is the leverage?

The leverage is in the shared foundation:

- monorepo structure
- shared framework
- shared design system
- operational dashboard

These reduce the cost of future iteration and help the team add capability without starting over each time.

### When is this most important?

At MVP stage. This is when a project needs to prove it can:

- ship
- operate
- evolve
- support investment into refinement and growth rather than reinvention

## Why the Current State Is Valuable

The strongest investor signal in this repository is not any one feature. It is the combination of:

- clear architecture
- documented workflows
- reusable components
- ongoing product refinement
- operational tooling inside the product itself

That combination suggests the team is building a platform, not just a demo.

## Scope Already Covered

The repository spans several layers of product value.

### Product Experience

The public app provides the external runtime for the Leida experience.

### Operations

The founder app supports internal business workflows such as:

- practitioner management
- affiliate product operations
- queue-based selection and review

### Platform Assets

The shared framework and design-system work provide long-term leverage:

- common runtime behavior
- common UI patterns
- shared theme and component assets
- centralized documentation

## Why This Lowers Execution Risk

A well-structured repository lowers several common startup risks:

- knowledge concentration in one person
- fragile one-off UI work
- duplicated code across product surfaces
- undocumented operational steps
- inability to onboard contributors efficiently

Leida has not eliminated all risk, but it has clearly reduced these specific categories.

## Evidence From Project Evolution

Recent commit history shows several healthy patterns:

- documentation was centralized and expanded
- the founder app was added and integrated into the repo structure
- design-system assets were extracted and improved
- AWIN workflows were scaled up and refined
- product shelf architecture was iterated instead of discarded
- route and card structures were improved as product flows matured

This is the kind of history you expect in a team moving from concept toward operational MVP.

## What “Solid Foundation” Means Here

In this repository, a solid foundation means:

- there is already a real internal operating surface
- the apps share architecture instead of drifting apart
- UI work is becoming systematized
- docs are good enough to support handover and review
- the codebase has a visible path to further polish

That is exactly the kind of work investors often need to know has already been done.

## Honest Current-State Framing

This is still an active MVP phase, not a finished end-state platform. Some flows are being refined, and parts of the product are still in motion. But the important point is that those refinements are happening on top of structure, documentation, and reusable assets.

The project is not trying to discover its shape anymore. It is sharpening it.

## Last-Week Momentum (2026-07-27 to 2026-08-03)

Recent commits reinforce execution momentum:

- public auth moved to a clearer Supabase session-gated model
- routine sharing/presentation UX materially improved
- API and type-shape refactoring continued around routine delivery
- design-system and Storybook maintenance continued alongside product work
- docs/media cleanup and asset pruning improved operational discipline

## Best Investor-Level Takeaway

> Leida has already completed a substantial layer of platform-building work across product experience, internal operations, architecture, and documentation, which puts the project in a stronger position to convert effort and funding into product refinement and growth rather than foundational rebuilds.
